# EU4 Random New World: lessons, not an EU5 contract

The [official Conquest of Paradise page](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/europa-universalis-iv/add-ons/europa-universalis-iv-conquest-of-paradise), accessed 2026-10-13, says EU4 can randomize the American continent and offers colonies and Native American nations [SOURCE, C16]. Its product description does not specify the generator algorithm, exposed settings, country distributions, rare fantasy outcomes, or whether generation is engine code versus script [UNKNOWN]. The linked [manual](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/241369/manuals/EuropaUniversalisIV_ConquestofParadise_Manual.pdf?t=1699879331) was not reviewed, so no mechanical claim is attributed to it. EU4's implementation must not be assumed to transfer to EU5.

The hypothesis that EU4 reuses American province identifiers is a useful T2 experiment design, but remains [UNKNOWN]; we have no primary source or binary evidence for it. The [RNW Adjustments Workshop item](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3125288376) is a community lead whose features, version, license and actual behavior were not verified in this session [SOURCE]. Therefore player complaints, the exact “fantasy” roster and rarity rules are also [UNKNOWN], rather than treated as established design facts.

What can be taken safely as **design principles** [INFERRED from C16 and the product goal]: preserve discovery, keep the Old World recognizably historical, make geography affect strategic decisions, and include both colonial and indigenous perspectives. The EU5 proposal goes beyond the official EU4 page by planning a shareable seed, a preview and reroll workflow, presets, coherent populations/economies/institutions, and uncommon extraordinary societies whose strength has simulation causes and weaknesses. These are product goals, not claims of engine support; see [product spec](../product-spec.md) and [feasibility](../feasibility.md).
