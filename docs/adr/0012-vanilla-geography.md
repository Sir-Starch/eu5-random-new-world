# ADR 0012: Keep the vanilla Americas, generate only their societies

**Status:** Accepted 2026-11-08 (owner's request, with the Omerika world type). Implementation
phases are tracked below; their acceptance is not a release hold for anything already shipped.

## Context

Every RNW world replaces the New World: the land, the cells, the relief, the rivers and the
location hierarchy are generated, and peoples, history and campaign content are built on them.
Omerika ([terrain](../terrain-biomes.md#omerika)) keeps only the *shape* of the Americas. The owner
also asked for an optional choice that changes **no geography at all**: the Americas stay exactly as
the game draws them, and only countries, cultures, religions, history and the rest of the societies
are generated.

The societies stage does not need generated land. `build_history_start` reads a `NewWorld` (cell
classes, contacts, pixel sizes, centres), a `Plan` (the key and the hierarchy of each cell), the
`CellBiomes` of each cell, river pixels and the vanilla calibration; the 1037-1337 simulation and the
projection into a start need nothing else. [V-EXEC 2026-11-08, EU5 1.4.7 / build 26377361] A spike
that wraps the installed Americas in those three types (6,669 cells: 4,434 land, 457 wasteland,
292 lakes, 1,479 seas) and calls the real `build_history_start` makes 482 countries and the
population of 4,434 land locations in 12 s.

## Decision

A world setting `new_world.keep_geography` (0 or 1, omitted from the settings document while 0, so
no code changes) makes the export a **societies-only export** on the vanilla map:

1. **The geography is the installation's.** Cells are the vanilla locations of the `america`
   continent plus the waters that touch them; keys, hierarchy, climate, vegetation, topography,
   resources, relief and rivers are read from the game. Nothing physical is generated or rewritten:
   no `locations.png`, `rivers.png`, terrain, vegetation, water, `definitions.txt`, `default.map`,
   location templates, ports, adjacencies, locators or named colours. The Old World and the New
   World both stay as the game has them.
2. **The societies are generated by the same layers.** Natives, states, cultures, religions, laws,
   technology, development of the start, AI, history, stories and formations are made by the existing
   stages, on the vanilla cells. Vanilla's own American setup (the countries and their locations,
   pops, buildings, diplomacy) is removed and replaced, as it is in every RNW world; scripts that
   name a removed country are remapped or retired by the existing machinery.
3. **Names.** Places keep the game's own names; no location text is written. Countries, cultures and
   religions are named by the generator as always.
4. **Layers.** *Peoples* can be rerolled; *resources* are the game's and have no roll. A land image,
   *Choose a continent* sketches, The Wall and the land controls do not apply; the window says so.
5. **One path, no fork of the engine.** A small adapter (`rnw.vanilla_world`) turns the installation
   into the types the stages already read; the export gains a second orchestrator
   (`rnw.eu5_vanilla_export`) that calls the shared society builders. The physical builders are not
   called. The generated-world path is byte-for-byte unchanged (reference digest).

## Consequences

- The mod holds only societal files, scripts, localization, metadata and previews. It is much smaller,
  and it cannot make a location, port or river that the game does not have.
- The map is recognisably vanilla: its quality is the game's. The generator's work is the people.
- Physical validators (seas, locators, contacts, canvas) have nothing to check and are not run; the
  society validators are the same ones.
- Engine behaviour of the new start is unobserved until an owner game sitting, as for every society
  change.

## Phases

| Phase | Content | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | `new_world.keep_geography` setting, world code field, validation (not with a drawn land or The Wall), the adapter `rnw.vanilla_world` with tests | A real-installation test builds the vanilla types; synthetic tests cover the adapter |
