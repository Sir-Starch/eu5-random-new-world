# Design and planning archive

These documents retain useful reasoning, rejected alternatives, measurements and
old experiment plans. They are **historical records**, not active instructions,
a current backlog or prerequisites for an accepted release. In particular, their
old version-bump rules, NOT-RUN checklists and early feasibility doubts do not
replace [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md) or [current guides](../README.md).

Numbered ADRs remain at their stable paths with explicit status and scope.
Research and experiment files remain in their own evidence collections. Preserve
their observations honestly; do not rewrite an old NOT-RUN into a claimed pass.
For new work, use the current implementation, later fixes and the owner's current
requirements to decide whether an old question is relevant.
