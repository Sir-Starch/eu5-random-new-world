# Random New World 1.3.0

RNW 1.3.0 expands the generator beyond the Americas with **optional generation of Africa and
Oceania** alongside or instead of America (with Eurasia work in progress), **natural wastelands**,
**river-first and climate-sized geography**, **compact sea cells**, a reworked **Terra Incognita**
generator appearance, **Valve Proton Experimental** support, and verification on **EU5 1.4.\***
(latest verified: **1.4.9 / Steam build 26655995**), using algorithm epoch **64**.

## 1. Optional continents: Africa and Oceania

- **Three independent continents:** Enable **America**, **Africa**, **Oceania**, or any
  combination in one world and one mod. Africa defaults to **Supercontinent** and Oceania to
  **Archipelago**, and each enabled continent has its own presets, six quick questions, Advanced
  controls, whole-map sketches and drawable land sheet in the land editor. **Keep the real
  Americas** can run alongside generated Africa and Oceania.
- **Morocco and the Mamluks:** When generating Africa, choose between four outcomes for Morocco's
  Iberian holdings (**Keep Morocco**, **Independent Algeciras**, **Join Castile**, **Join
  Granada**) and two for the Mamluks' Asian holdings (**Keep Mamluks**, **Independent Sultanate
  of Damascus**). Restored Morocco (86 African holdings) and Mamluks (68 holdings) grow as
  connected territories and carry over the pops, towns, markets, institutions and country
  buildings from their 1337 African holdings.
- **Land bridges to Asia and Iberia:** Africa's canvas reaches the Arabian shore, with optional or
  required winding land bridges at the original border or along Arabia. **Allow a land connection
  to Iberia (no strait)** lets a seed close the Strait of Gibraltar by land (with a sub-option to
  require it), turning the Mediterranean into an enclosed sea; exports still succeed even when both
  Mediterranean ends are closed.
- **Continuous seas and shared oceans:** The Mediterranean keeps its basin and area names along
  the new African coast, open-Atlantic island sites are released when both America and Africa are
  generated, Oceanian archipelagos stay together on the Pacific side, and **Normal** land adjusts
  location spacing per continent (`66 px` for America alone or `51 px` shared, `66 px` Africa,
  `72 px` Oceania) to stay close to vanilla playable location counts.

## 2. Natural wastelands, river-first geography and compact seas

- **Natural wastelands:** Impassable locations are planned once climate and vegetation are drawn,
  dividing the wasteland budget between dunes, plateaus, hills, flat wilds (tundra, taiga, jungle)
  and mountains according to vanilla terrain and climate shares. Wastelands are named by terrain
  (*Highlands*, *Wilds*, *Desert*) across all eleven languages, and every passable location keeps
  a path to the sea.
- **River-first, climate-sized land:** Active coasts raise cordilleras, seasonal rainfall (summer,
  winter, monsoons and cold subtropical currents) feeds drainage before land cells are formed, and
  larger rivers become location borders. Locations are sized by their climate, vegetation and
  ground and graded smoothly across neighbours.
- **Pooled raw materials:** Small climate/vegetation/terrain classes blend with broader vanilla
  classes so rare habitats no longer miss food or mines while vanilla totals remain calibrated.
- **Compact, even sea cells:** Open water uses a regular grid with local partitions along shores
  and through narrow straits so sea zones no longer stretch across straits or wrap crookedly
  around islands.
- **Sea route around the world:** **Keep a sea route around the world** checks the finished
  continents and opens a narrow channel through generated land only if no navigable route exists,
  instead of reserving a strip along the southern map edge.

## 3. Playable cities, balanced peoples and valid starts

- **City-grid size bound:** Passable land locations stay under 15,000–20,000 pixels by splitting
  oversized cells during growth, preventing the engine crash when opening city details on
  oversized locations.
- **Plan-based populations:** Location populations scale by the planned cell area rather than
  final pixel count, keeping cities and towns distributed across normal-sized locations instead of
  the largest cells.
- **Laws, reforms and rivers:** Marriage and heir religion law readers handle vanilla `!=`, `or`
  and `not` conditions, Australian cultures no longer receive Polynesian-only reforms, and
  retained rivers cut from their sources end cleanly at the replaced continent's edge.

## 4. Generator window, Terra Incognita and performance

- **Terra Incognita appearance:** Settings → Appearance adds **Terra Incognita**, an aged chart
  look with foxed paper grain, leather-dark panels, gilt frames, engraved corners, brass-plate
  buttons, compass-rose empty sheets and custom-styled drop-down menus.
- **Reworked multi-continent window and classic Qt parity:** Whole-map sketches, per-continent
  land editor sheets, nine-phase progress tracking, lit-sheet physical maps with kept Old World
  lands and continent labels, and full feature parity in the classic Qt fallback window.
- **Valve Proton Experimental and smoother exports:** The Workshop/Proton hub includes an
  `icuuc.dll` forwarder (`generator/compat/icuuc.dll`) so the classic window opens under Valve's
  Proton Experimental on Linux and Steam Deck; NumPy huge-page requests are disabled to stop
  machine-wide stalls during exports; and native Rust parsers and bounded strip kernels speed up
  generation with unchanged world bytes.

## 5. Compatibility, epochs and updating

Generate a new world to use the new continents, geography and epoch **64**. **Experimental
features** in Settings lets you choose epoch **63** (1.2.2), **62** (1.2.1) or **61** (1.2.0) on
your installed game files; existing world mods and saves are not rewritten or migrated.

Use the exact EU5 version that made a campaign's map (**EU5 1.4.\***, latest verified: **1.4.9 /
build 26655995**; most patches within the 1.4 branch work without a generator update). Enable
**one generated RNW world** at a time (keep the generator hub disabled),
allow about **8 GB of free RAM** for generation, and restart EU5 before switching maps. Under
Valve's Proton, use **Proton Experimental** (or **GE-Proton**); Valve's Proton 10.0 lacks a math
function required by NumPy.

## 6. Downloads and help

- **Windows:** standalone generator archive.
- **Workshop-Windows-Proton:** generator hub for Workshop and Linux/Proton use.
- **Linux:** native application archive.
- **Source:** project source under MPL-2.0.

Verify downloads against the attached `SHA256SUMS`. These packages contain no game assets; worlds
are generated locally from your EU5 installation. Windows binaries are unsigned, so Smart App
Control or antivirus software may prompt; the macOS archive is a CI build, not an offered game
route.
