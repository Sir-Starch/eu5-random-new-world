# Random New World 1.2.1

RNW 1.2.1 is a hotfix for 1.2.0. In a generated 1.2.0 world almost every country of the New World
had a market of its own. 1.2.1 puts the market centres back where the settlement plan places them,
as in 1.1.0 and as the game's own Americas have them.

This release targets **EU5 1.4.7 / Steam build 26377361**, with algorithm epoch **62**.

## 1. What was wrong

1.2.0 turned every country that had recorded an exchange agreement in its history into a market
centre and a town. In the installed 1.2.0 worlds 70–87 % of the generated capitals held a market;
one world had 115 market centres and 113 towns for 127 countries. The game's Americas have 12
market centres for 223 landed countries.

## 2. What changed

- An exchange agreement leaves an opinion between its partners and land roads inside their
  territories, and nothing else. It no longer creates a market centre or a town.
- Market centres and towns are the calibrated ones again: the cities, the largest state near each
  hearth, and one local market for each group of countries that no market reaches. The same world
  has 18 market centres instead of 115.
- Because the towns changed, so did what depends on them: the start development, the AI's
  personalities, and the texts and scripts that name a country's centres. 27 of the world's 884
  files differ from 1.2.0's; the count is unchanged.
- The check before generation asks for about **9 GB** of free RAM, the measured need of a 1.2.0
  world (its smallest run peaks at 8.1 GiB), and says how much is free when it stops.

## 3. Compatibility and saves

Generate a new world to get the corrected markets. Existing worlds and saves keep their own files
and algorithm epoch; updating the generator does not rewrite them, and a campaign continues with
its exact world mod.

A world code made by 1.2.0 (algorithm epoch 61) can give another world in 1.2.1 (epoch 62), and the
generator refuses new peoples, new resources and *Same land, change settings* on a world made by an
earlier epoch, with the reason shown. Use **New world with these settings** to get a new land.

Enable **one generated RNW world** at a time; the Workshop item supplies the generator, not a
world. Restart EU5 before switching maps. Allow about **9 GB of free RAM** for generation.

## 4. Downloads and help

- **Windows:** standalone generator archive.
- **Workshop-Windows-Proton:** generator hub for Workshop and Linux/Proton use.
- **Linux:** native application archive.
- **Source:** project source under MPL-2.0.

Verify downloads against the attached `SHA256SUMS`. These packages contain no game assets; worlds
are generated locally from your EU5 installation.
