# 024 — Can the terrain layer tell the selection screen from a campaign?

- **Status:** **RUN 2026-10-21 (C135).** The tint was cyan at selection and in the campaign, and it lay over the mirror image of the New World: the grid is flipped north–south, the terrain reads TI, and at selection every location reads as unknown. Prepared the same day (generator 0.9.17) after experiment 023, run 2 (C134).
- **Cleaned up 2026-10-22:** `build.py` (the probe's builder) and `fit.py` (the grid fit) were deleted with `generated/rnw_exp_probe_cover` once the cover worked (experiment 023, run 5, C139). The paragraphs below describe what they did; the results stand as recorded (C135).
- **Why:** run 2 of experiment 023 loaded the cover (it is compiled in the shader cache) and covered nothing: the TI include is used only by the TI overlay and the borders, and the TI overlay is not drawn at country selection (C134). A cover at selection must then be drawn by layers that are drawn there, the terrain first. Two things decide whether that can work: whether the terrain shader can read the TI visibility texture (a `Ref` texture, as the terrain's own `SeaColors`), and whether at selection that texture reports the world's sample locations as known (C127: with no viewing country every location is known) while in a campaign it does not. The probe also shows where the world's cover grid lies on the map.
- **The probe** (`rnw_exp_probe_cover`, built locally, never committed: it is the game's terrain shader with additions): `uv run python experiments/024-terrain-cover-probe/build.py GAME_DIR WORLD_MOD generated/rnw_exp_probe_cover` copies the cover grid and the eight sample points from the world's own include (`rnw_exp_world_dsllwsx2jw`) into `main_menu/gfx/FX/pdxterrain2.shader` and tints the terrain over the grid's cells: **magenta** when all eight sample locations read as known, **cyan** when one does not; lighter where the pixel's own location reads as known. Nothing else changes; the world mod and its cover stay as they are.
- **Method (owner):** with EU5 closed, install the probe: `! uv run rnw install-mod generated/rnw_exp_probe_cover "<steam-library>/steamapps/compatdata/3450310/pfx/drive_c/users/steamuser/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Europa Universalis V"`; in the launcher tick it in the RNW playset beside the world (order does not matter, no other mod changes this file). Start EU5, start a new 1337 game, and at the country-selection screen, before choosing anything, note the colour over the New World (political lens and terrain lens, close and far). Then choose an Old World country, start, and note the colour and where it lies against the New World's coasts.
- **What each outcome means:**
  - Magenta at selection, cyan in the campaign (lighter where explored): the terrain can tell selection from a campaign with the world's sample locations, and can read per-location TI; the cover moves into the layers drawn at selection (terrain, flat map, water, borders, names).
  - Cyan at selection too: the texture does not report the samples as known at selection (it is not filled there, or not bound for the terrain); another way to tell selection apart is needed.
  - Magenta in the campaign as well: the sample locations read as known there; the samples or the reading are wrong.
  - No tint anywhere: the terrain shader of the probe is not used, or the grid lies off the map; a shader message in `error.log` says which.
  - The tint lies beside the New World rather than over it: the grid is mirrored or shifted; its orientation is fixed in `rnw.eu5_lobby`.
- **What I read afterwards** (read only): `error.log` for shader messages, the shader cache for a new `pdxterrain2` entry.
- **Expected changes in the profile:** `mod/rnw_exp_probe_cover` (new, 2 files, about 20 KB). Removal with the game closed: untick it, then `uv run rnw uninstall-mod rnw_exp_probe_cover --user-dir USER_DIR`.
- **Game version:** 1.4.2 open beta, build 26043558 (C84).

## Run (owner, 2026-10-21 21:46-21:53)

- **Loaded:** the probe was ticked in the game's own playset manager at 21:47:32, which reloaded the mods fully (loading-screen transitions); `debug.log` lists it enabled, last, from then on, and the shader cache holds 34 pixel shaders with `RnwProbe`, compiled at 21:48. Experiment 022's probe was still enabled; it changes no shader. The first lobby, at 21:46, ran without the probe.
- **Seen (owner, five screenshots):** cyan, close to turquoise, over a blocky region that is not the New World: in the political lens it covers much of the Atlantic and Pacific and parts of the New World, in the 3D map only land (the water is another shader), with blocks on Greenland, Iberia, Morocco and the Algerian Sahara (Touat, Mzab). As Portugal in the campaign the colour is the same where it shows, over the Iberian and Moroccan coast; the New World is under the ordinary grey TI.
- **Where the grid lies:** `uv run python experiments/024-terrain-cover-probe/fit.py WORLD_FXH SCREENSHOT` on the zoomed-out political screenshot of the lobby: map width 1490 px on screen (5.82 px per cell); the grid **flipped north–south** fits the cyan with IoU **0.959**, as built at most 0.41 (even when allowed to slide down a third of the map). The vanilla shaders agree: world z grows northwards and image rows southwards (`pdxterrain2.fxh` and `terrain.fxh` read the flat map and the tint at `1 - y`; `pdxwater.shader` takes `1 - z / size`). The grid and the eight sample points of 0.9.16 and 0.9.17 pointed at the mirror image of the New World.
- **What the terrain reads:** the colours untouched by the tint are equal in the lobby and the campaign (Castile 130,105,52 and 130,104,47; sea 78,95,107 and 77,93,106), so the two screenshots are drawn alike. Over the tinted Iberia the green and blue channels moved about 0.78 of the way to cyan in the lobby and about 0.45 in the campaign, the probe's two strengths for an unknown (0.7) and a known (0.35) location through the game's tone curve. So **the terrain shader reads the TI visibility texture** (Portugal's own lands read as known in the campaign), and **at selection the same locations read as unknown**. With the samples mirrored the magenta test said nothing, but the per-pixel reading answers the question better: at selection everything is unknown, not known.
- **Outcome:** neither row of the table above. Selection needs no detector: a cover drawn where a location is in the New World and unknown is the game's own TI rule; in a campaign the TI overlay draws over the same places, and at selection, where the overlay is not drawn, the map's layers must draw it. The owner chose the four layers seen in the screenshots: terrain (3D and flat map), water, borders and names (C136).
- **Removal:** the probe overrides the terrain shader and so would hide the next world's cover.
