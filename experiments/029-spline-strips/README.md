Date prepared: 2026-10-22. **PARTIAL: 0.9.30 loaded with no strip error and no crash; the owner first reported no roads and then withdrew it (wrong lens, C166); roads draw with 0.9.31 (C166).**

- **Hypothesis:** EU5 1.4.3 reads a `spline_network.splnet` that a mod puts at the vanilla path, made by `rnw.eu5_splines` (C162, C163). Then (a) the log has none of the `Could not find spline network strip of type 0 between anchors` lines of C161 for generated locations, set-up roads and roads built in play alike; (b) the roads of the empires draw on the map from the capital to the vassals' capitals and the largest locations; (c) the vanilla roads of the Old World look as before; (d) the game starts as it did, with no crash and no new error lines naming `spline`, `anchor` or `rnw_`.
- **Method**, on the installed world below (the playset needs hub + this world; EU5 started by the owner, nothing else changed):
  1. Start a 1337 game of the world and let it run at speed for a few minutes (the flood of C161 began when time ran). Count `spline network strip` lines in `debug.log`, which holds the whole session (`error.log` rotates every 15–20 s when it floods). Copy both logs before the next launch.
  2. Look at the roads of an empire (tags below) on the map and in the road map mode: from the capital to the capitals of its vassals and to the biggest location of each area it holds.
  3. Build a road between two generated locations of a state (and let the AI build some): the road should draw, with no strip error.
  4. Look at two vanilla roads in the Old World, for example Stockholm–Uppsala and Dublin–Naas.
  5. Compare the start-up time with the previous session of 0.9.29 (22:51–22:52 on 2026-10-22).
