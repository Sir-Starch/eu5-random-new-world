# 031 — The cover's drawings of terra incognita, and the lobby's list without a gap

Date prepared: 2026-10-23. **NOT-RUN** (generator 0.10.0, installed as `rnw_exp_world_cyhlrdiik7`; NOT GAME VALIDATED).

- **Context:** [V-GAME owner, 2026-10-23] shaders and the hiding of the New World look right in the latest run; two things remain: the covered New World is a flat colour without the game's drawings (dragons, compasses), and the lobby's list of countries has long empty stretches where the hidden countries stand (two screenshots, top and bottom of the list, not committed). C172.
- **Hypothesis A (drawings):** `terra_incognita.shader` reads its drawings through `TerraIncognitaWorldTexture { Index = 2, file = "gfx/map/flatmap/terra_incognita.dds" }` [V-LOCAL: the texture is 16,384 x 8,192, DXT1; the output is multiplied by `1 - 0.75 r` and `1 - 0.5 g`, UV = the world position over the map's size, y flipped]. A sampler declared the same way by a mod's include (`rnw_new_world_drawings.fxh`) in `pdxterrain2.shader` and `pdxwater.shader`, the two shaders that paint the unknown colour, is bound to the game's texture, with no copy of it in the mod, so at country selection the covered New World shows the drawings as the game's own terra incognita does in a campaign. No sampler of either shader's include graph (50 includes each) holds an `Index` [V-LOCAL], and the export refuses a shader where another sampler would hold Index 2 (`binding_problem`), as it refuses a slot named twice (C138).
- **Hypothesis B (list):** the list is a `fixedgridbox` of cells of 74 pixels over `AccessLobbyCountryList.GetCountries`; a hidden item (C150's `visible` condition) keeps its cell, which is the gap. The list asks for the scripted filters of the tag `no_filters` (`WithFilterTags('no_filters')`, once in the lobby GUI), and no vanilla filter has that tag [V-LOCAL]. A filter of the mod (`in_game/gui/filters/99_rnw_new_world.txt`: scope country, tag `no_filters`, on from the start, hidden in the search bar, passing every country whose capital is not on the New World's continent) leaves the New World's countries out of the list itself. The item condition stays as the guard for the case that the player unticks the filter.
- **Method** (the playset needs the hub and this world only; EU5 started by the owner; nothing else changed):
  1. At country selection look at the covered New World from far out and close, on the flat map and in 3D, over land and over the covered sea: do dragons and compasses show, as in the game's own terra incognita (e.g. over the Old World's unknown edges of a campaign)? Is anything dark, striped or garbled that is not a drawing?
  2. Scroll the list of countries to its top and its end, change the sort order, type a few letters in the search: no empty stretch, no New World country. Open the list's filter menu (the search bar's menu) if there is one: is there a filter named *New World hidden*?
  3. Start a game with an Old World country and look at the New World under the game's own terra incognita: unchanged.
  4. Copy `error.log` and `debug.log`: no shader line, no `filter` line naming `rnw_new_world_hidden`, no missing-key line for `search_filter_rnw_new_world_hidden_*`.
- **Game version:** 1.4.3 open beta, build 26105915 [V-LOCAL C148].
- **Outcomes and what each decides:**
  - Drawings show, list without a gap: done; the cover is complete.
  - **No drawings, no crash:** the game does not bind an `Index` sampler in these shaders' draws. Next: a `Ref` slot (`FlatMap2` is declared by no vanilla shader; the `DynamicTerrainMask` slots 0 to 13 are all taken in the two shaders' graphs and no mod can tell whether the engine shares them), probed on a world of its own.
  - **Garbled or dark patterns over the New World:** the engine binds another texture at Index 2 in one of the draws. Next: another Index per shader.
  - **Crash at the first frame of the map:** the failure kind of C137 and C138; the shader cache shows which shader did not compile. 0.10.1 returns to the flat colour (no drawings) and keeps the list filter.
  - **List: the New World's countries show:** the filter was not applied (look for a script error of the filter in `error.log`); the item guard still hides them, so the gap is back, as in 0.9.33.
  - **List: the gap remains and no country shows:** the filter was not applied; same cause.
- **Offline results [V-EXEC]:** the include's code compiles to SPIR-V with `glslangValidator` with the drawings' sampler passed in; the real game's two shaders pass `binding_problems` with the new include (`tests/test_local_install.py`), and that test also checks that `terra_incognita.shader` declares the sampler as assumed, that the texture exists, that the lobby asks for the tag once and that no vanilla filter has it. The new world differs from the 0.9.33 world of the same code in `pdxterrain2.shader`, `pdxwater.shader`, `rnw_new_world_cover.fxh`, the drawings' include, the filter file, ten localization files of its texts and `.metadata`.

The engine's own binding of an `Index` sampler outside the terra incognita overlay is not documented; the evidence is the one precedent (the overlay's own shader, and `tree.shader`'s `TintMap { Index = 3 }`), not a test. Whether the lobby shows filters of the tag `no_filters` in a menu, and with what text, is also unknown.
